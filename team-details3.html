Biography

Vincent Obi is a seasoned ICT and cybersecurity leader with over 14 years of experience in securing enterprise systems, driving digital transformation, and managing cyber risk. He has led high-impact security initiatives, including implementing the ISMA Security Framework for Heineken Sierra Leone, and brings deep expertise in risk management, IT governance, compliance, and advanced network security. A CISM-certified professional, Vincent specializes in firewall management, enterprise network design, virtualization, and secure cloud and server environments. Known for his strategic leadership and hands-on technical expertise, he consistently delivers resilient, scalable, and secure ICT infrastructures while empowering teams and strengthening organizational cyber defenses.
