Frequently Asked Questions

What types of businesses need cybersecurity solutions?

Every business, regardless of size or industry, is a potential target for cyber threats. Whether you're a startup, SME, or large enterprise, cybersecurity helps protect your data, systems, and reputation.

How do your cybersecurity services protect against ransomware and phishing attacks?

We deploy advanced threat detection tools, email filtering, endpoint protection, and employee training programs to prevent and respond to ransomware, phishing, and other cyber threats effectively.

Do you offer 24/7 threat monitoring and incident response?

Yes. Our Security Operations Center (SOC) provides 24/7 monitoring, real-time threat detection, and rapid incident response to ensure your business is always protected.

Can your solutions help us meet compliance requirements like GDPR or ISO 27001?

Absolutely. Our services include compliance assessments, policy development, and implementation support to help you meet regulatory standards such as GDPR, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and more.
