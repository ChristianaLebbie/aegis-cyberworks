Years of Experience

Your Partner for Cyber Security Solution

Aegis CyberWorks is a dynamic cybersecurity partner helping organizations across Africa and beyond build trust in their digital operations. Rooted in national CSIRT experience, we understand the realities of defending systems and data in complex, fast-evolving environments.

We collaborate with companies, government institutions, and innovators in Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Cabo Verde, and Abu Dhabi, with more partnerships steadily growing across the continent. Our expertise spans security audits, digital forensics, anti-surveillance measures, secure systems design, and incident response capability development — all tailored to the needs of emerging markets.

Our mission is to strengthen Africa’s digital resilience through collaboration, innovation, and solutions that meet global standards while addressing local realities.
